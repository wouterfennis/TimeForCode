A store you want to browse

Moodboard for the toned-down game-store direction in design-system.md. Built in code with the tokens the SPA will use (#211). Project data and hours are sample values; images come from GitHub's generated Open Graph images (the interim source in #213).

References and what we take from them

  • Steam store

    Dark surfaces, image-first capsules, shelves by theme, badges that carry the key fact.

  • PlayStation 5 interface

    Tiles that respond to focus with a small lift and glow; calm, quick motion.

  • Polar

    Developer funding presented in a modern, restrained dark UI.

  • Open Collective

    Transparent progress: who gave what, and how much is still needed.

  • Catchafire

    Skills-based volunteering: the "what help is needed" framing.

We take patterns, not looks: no copied colours, logos or layouts. And no store tricks: no countdowns, fake urgency or scarcity.
